# Career Blogs

This folder is for reflective, career-positioning blogs that explain the professional path behind the portfolio.

These posts are different from the main 10-blog roadmap. The main roadmap proves topical authority around CMS, frontend, SEO/QA, localization, and AI workflows. Career blogs explain the story behind the work: how the experience developed, what patterns emerged, and where the career is going next.

## Purpose

Career blogs help recruiters, hiring managers, and collaborators understand:

- how my interest in content management development grew
- how my roles shaped my current direction
- why I connect frontend development, CMS operations, marketing technology, and workflow systems
- what kind of work I want to keep building toward

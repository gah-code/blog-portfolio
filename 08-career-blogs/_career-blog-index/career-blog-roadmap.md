# Career Blog Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap tracks career-story content separate from the main topical blog roadmap.

Career blogs should answer:

- How did I get into this work?
- What roles shaped my direction?
- What patterns connect my experience?
- What kind of web technology work am I building toward?

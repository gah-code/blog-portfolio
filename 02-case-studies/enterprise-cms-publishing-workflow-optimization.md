# Enterprise CMS Publishing & Workflow Optimization

## Overview

Supported large-scale CMS migration and publishing operations across international marketing environments by improving workflow visibility, QA structure, and handoff clarity.

## The Problem

Migration work can become scattered when pages, redirects, QA checks, stakeholder approvals, localization needs, and content status live in too many places.

Without a clearer system, teams risk duplicate work, unclear ownership, and late-stage launch friction.

## My Role

Supported migration execution, content QA, SEO review, redirect validation, cross-device UAT, stakeholder coordination, and workflow improvement.

## Process

1. Reviewed migration workflow needs.
2. Identified redundant task tracking.
3. Consolidated overlapping lists and sheets.
4. Introduced semi-automated tracking patterns.
5. Standardized handoff steps.
6. Supported QA/UAT and launch-readiness checks.

## Solution

Created a clearer workflow for tracking content status, ownership, blockers, QA needs, and launch readiness.

## Outcome

Helped improve delivery speed, reduce redundant work, and support stronger operational throughput.

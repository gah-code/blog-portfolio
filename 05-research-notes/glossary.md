# Glossary

## AEM

Adobe Experience Manager, an enterprise CMS used for managing digital experiences and web content.

## CMS Operations

The workflows, standards, QA steps, metadata, publishing rules, and coordination required to manage content inside a CMS.

## Launch Readiness

The state where content, QA, SEO, redirects, accessibility, approvals, and publishing dependencies are complete enough to release.

## Metadata

Structured information that helps describe, organize, categorize, find, and optimize content.

## UAT

User Acceptance Testing. A review process to confirm that a page, workflow, or feature meets business and user expectations.

## Localization

The process of adapting content, imagery, metadata, and page structures for different regions, languages, and markets.

## AI-Assisted Reporting

Using AI tools to support repeated reporting tasks such as summaries, status updates, documentation drafts, and ticket lifecycle visibility.
